🛠️ Problem Statement (Production-Style ETL Pipeline)
You are a data engineer at an e-commerce company.

Every day, the company receives raw order data files (like the ones in the Olist dataset).

These files are messy: they may have missing values, inconsistent formats, or errors.

Analysts and business teams need clean, reliable data in a database or warehouse to run reports (sales, revenue, customer behavior).

Your job:
Build a Python ETL pipeline that:

Extracts raw order data from daily files.

Validates & Transforms the data (fix schema issues, clean timestamps, calculate derived fields).

Loads the cleaned data into a database (e.g., PostgreSQL, MySQL, or even SQLite for practice).

Runs automatically every day and keeps logs of what was processed.

🎯 Business Goal
Ensure that business teams always have accurate daily order data.

Enable reporting on KPIs like:

Daily sales totals

Payment methods used

Delivery times

Customer satisfaction (reviews)

🔑 Why This Matters
In real companies, raw data is never directly usable.

Analysts don’t want to deal with messy CSVs.

Executives want dashboards updated daily.

Data engineers make this possible by building robust pipelines that transform chaos into clean, structured data.
